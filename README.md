# Code-Alpha-Internship-Phishing-Awareness-Training

Phishing Awareness TrainingOverviewThis training module focuses on recognizing, understanding, and defending against phishing emails. It combines theory with real-world case studies to highlight red flags and best practices.What Is Phishing?Phishing is a social engineering attack where adversaries impersonate trusted entities (banks, employers, brands) to trick victims into revealing sensitive information or clicking malicious links.Social Engineering TacticsUrgency: “Your account will be suspended in 24 hours.”Authority: Fake messages from banks or employers.Fear: Threats of closure or financial loss.Curiosity & Reward: Fake prizes or invoices.Trust Exploitation: Impersonating colleagues/vendors.Scarcity: “Only 2 spots left.”Case StudiesLegitimate Email (RedCloud Recruitment)SPF, DKIM, DMARC all passed.Sender traced to Amazon SES.Consistent domain family, clean links.Calm, personalized tone.Phishing Email (Amazon Impersonation)Generic greeting, urgency, vague sender.SPF/DMARC failed.Origin traced to unrelated domain.Redirect + URL shortener → fake login page.Spotting Fake WebsitesCheck the address bar, not just design.Watch for typosquatting (e.g., arnazon.com, amaz0n-login.com).Padlock icon ≠ legitimacy.Type known addresses directly instead of clicking links.Best PracticesVerify independently: Use official apps/sites.Enable MFA: Protects even if password is stolen.Never log in via email links.Report suspicious emails to IT/security.Confirm requests via separate channels.Practice regularly to sharpen detection skills.Knowledge CheckUrgency/fear are common phishing tactics.Legitimate emails pass SPF/DKIM/DMARC.Phishing links often hide behind redirects.MFA is the strongest protection against stolen credentials.ConclusionPhishing remains one of the most common cyberattack entry points.
